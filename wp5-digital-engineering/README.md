# WP5: Digital engineering

**Owner:** Group 5

## Purpose

Provide and validate the formal bridge between the business canvas, the behaviour diagram, the system model and the environmental assessment, so that consistency between them is a machine-checked fact.

## What goes in this folder

Shared vocabulary and ontology, the consistency pipeline and its defect tests, the consistency report.
