# WP4: System dynamics

**Owner:** Group 4

## Purpose

Model how the service business behaves over time, and prove or disprove that the regenerative effects compound over thirty years.

## What goes in this folder

Economic, social and environmental behaviour diagrams, the integrated diagram, the parameterised model, loop catalogue.
